# 01. Competition Brief
## Source of truth and official objective
OFFICIAL PDF. Healthkathon 2026 seeks digital innovation to identify, detect, and mitigate risks in JKN healthcare services, improving effectiveness, efficiency, service quality, and digital transformation. The official theme is “Detect Smarter, Protect JKN — Smarter Protection for a Stronger JKN: Efisiensi Risiko Program JKN.” The official challenge is “Efisiensi Risiko pada Pelayanan Kesehatan Program JKN.” [PDF-PG pp. 2–3]
## Official categories and combination rule
| Official category | Officially listed risk modes — concise extraction |
|---|---|
| Efisiensi Risiko pada Peserta | participant data/identity falsification; misuse/loan/rental/sale of identity; medically unnecessary services; misuse of drugs/medical devices. [PDF-PG category table] |
| Efisiensi Risiko pada Fasilitas Kesehatan | capitation/non-capitation misuse; claim manipulation; improper referral; upcoding; cloning; phantom billing; inflated bills; episode splitting; unbundling/fragmentation; self-referral; repeat billing; prolonged length of stay; room-charge manipulation; billing for services not performed; non-indicated treatment; readmission; fictitious drugs/devices/procedures; reduced dispensed quantity; manipulated test results. [PDF-PG pp. 6–7] |
| Efisiensi Risiko pada Pemberi Kerja | partial worker registration; underreported wages; withheld/delayed remittance; employment-status misclassification; manipulation of new/mutated/resigned worker data; and, in the Participant Guide, collusion with facilities/fictitious medical certificates. [PDF-PG p. 7] |


Non-negotiable: a team selects exactly one official category. It may address one or more modes only inside that selected category. A team submits one innovation. [PDF-PG; PDF-PP p. 2]
## Proposal requirements and constraints
| Requirement | Binding constraint |
|---|---|
| Format | PDF |
| Maximum length | 20 slides |
| Maximum file size | 10 MB |
| Category/solution | One official category and one solution per team |
| Required content | Identity and positioning; problem and urgency; solution and advantage; technical/data approach; maturity/prototype; implementation plan and feasibility; impact/value; risk/privacy/ethics; team profile |

Source: [PDF-PP pp. 2, 6–14].
## Official six judging dimensions
The proposal guide publishes six dimensions but does not publish weights. Do not invent official weights.
- Relevance of the problem — real, specific, urgent, and tied to the JKN challenge.
- Creativity and innovation — meaningful advantage, not novelty theater.
- Technology quality — coherent end-to-end design and appropriate use of AI.
- Effectiveness and impact — measurable value and plausible change.
- Readiness and scalability — honest maturity, feasible plan, and path to JKN-scale use.
- Security and governance — privacy, ethics, risk control, explainability, and human oversight.
Source: [PDF-PP p. 3].

## Eligibility, originality, data, and conduct
- Teams contain one to three members, with one chair. Each person may join one team, and each team submits one innovation. [PDF-PG pp. 4–5]
- Participation is open to the public except BPJS Kesehatan employees; participants must commit to all stages, which may be hybrid. [PDF-PG p. 4]
- Work must be original and must not violate intellectual property, contain prohibited/illegal content, or include SARA. [PDF-PG pp. 4–5]
- Real JKN participant data may not be used without official authorization. Use dummy/simulated data or anonymized data in accordance with the rules. [PDF-PG p. 5]
- Data privacy, security, and confidentiality must be maintained. The proposal guide expects explicit treatment of UU PDP, anonymous/synthetic data, bias, ethics, and human oversight. [PDF-PG p. 5; PDF-PP p. 13]
- Fraud, cheating, false identity, or false documentation can lead to score reduction, disqualification, or revocation of awards. [PDF-PG p. 9]
- Scores are confidential except for grand finalists; official rules may change. [PDF-PG pp. 5, 9]

# 24. Final Submission Checklist
## Competition compliance
☐  Registration confirmed; all identities accurate; team size 1–3; chair identified.
☐  One official category: Efisiensi Risiko pada Fasilitas Kesehatan.
☐  One solution; selected modes all belong to facility category.
☐  Disputed category mode excluded.
☐  Proposal submission completed by 19 September; internal upload on 18 September.
☐  PDF; 16 recommended slides; never above 20; file at or below 10 MB.
☐  All nine official proposal components and six judging dimensions mapped.
☐  Originality, libraries, datasets, fonts, icons, and visual licenses documented.
## Evidence and claim quality
☐  Every factual claim has an official PDF, authoritative web, regulatory, or peer-reviewed source.
☐  Webinar themes are labeled verbal strategic signals, not official categories.
☐  Analysis and assumptions are visibly distinct from facts.
☐  No national fraud prevalence/loss claim unless a reliable source is added.
☐  No production BPJS/SATUSEHAT integration claim.
☐  Every result comes from a committed run artifact; no placeholder survives.
☐  All synthetic tables/charts/screenshots say SYNTHETIC PROTOTYPE DATA.
☐  Results state what they demonstrate and do not demonstrate.
☐  Prototype/MVP terminology is honest.
## Product and technical proof
☐  Clean local start, seed, screen, review, audit, and reset succeed.
☐  At least three modes work end-to-end at Gate 4; four at Gate 6 or scope is transparently narrowed.
☐  Every reason resolves to source resources/derived edges.
☐  Human disposition requires a reason and logs a versioned event.
☐  Rules-only baseline and hybrid comparison are reproducible.
☐  Leakage probes and grouped splits pass.
☐  Error, empty, loading, partial, and stale-case states are handled.
☐  No external service is required for the demo.
## Privacy/governance
☐  No unauthorized real JKN participant data.
☐  IDs are pseudonymous and no names/NIK appear.
☐  Logs contain no raw health text.
☐  UI uses risk/anomaly language, not accusation.
☐  AI cannot reject, sanction, alter codes, or decide medical necessity.
☐  Counter-evidence and incomplete-bundle uncertainty are visible.
☐  Data/model cards, audit design, prohibited use, and threat model exist.
## Deck and file QA
☐  Slide titles form a coherent story when read alone.
☐  Fonts are embedded or safely substituted; no clipped text.
☐  Charts remain readable at presentation size and use consistent denominators.
☐  URLs and citations are legible; all links checked.
☐  File opens in two viewers and on a second machine if possible.
☐  File name is professional and versioned; final hash recorded.
☐  Portal preview matches local PDF.
☐  Submission receipt/status and timestamp are saved.
## Later-stage readiness
☐  90-second and 3-minute demos rehearsed.
☐  Release tag, recorded fallback, and screenshot fallback archived.
☐  Expert/organizer questions ready.
☐  Pilot metrics, data request, and architecture assumptions ready for coaching.
